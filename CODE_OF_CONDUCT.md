# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes respectful language, constructive feedback,
gracefully accepting criticism, focusing on the community's interests, and
showing empathy. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling,
derogatory comments, sexualized attention, personal attacks, or publishing
another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to `daniel@eidosagi.com`. Maintainers will review
reports privately and respond proportionately. Maintainers who do not follow
this code may be removed from project responsibilities.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.0.
